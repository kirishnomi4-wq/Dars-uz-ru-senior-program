KOD · TO‘LIQ CRUD

O‘zgartirish, o‘chirish va “topilmadi”

NATIJA



CRUD — Create, Read, Update, Delete: deyarli har qanday loyihaning asosi. Bu yerda yetishmagan ikki eshik (PATCH, DELETE) va ikki muhim odat: ParseIntPipe bilan id’ni songa aylantirish va topilmasa NotFoundException tashlash. Service’dagi update va remove metodlari: update — findOne qilib, Object.assign(car, body); remove — findIndex va splice.
