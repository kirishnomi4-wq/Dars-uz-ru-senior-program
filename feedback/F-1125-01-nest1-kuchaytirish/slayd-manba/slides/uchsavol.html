ERROR [ExceptionHandler] Nest can't resolve dependencies of the CarsController (?). Please make sure that the argument CarsService at index [0] is available in the CarsModule context.
1. QAYSI KLASS?
CarsController
«of the ___» — kim yordam so‘rayapti
2. QAYSI DEPENDENCY?
CarsService
«argument ___» — nima topilmadi
3. QAYSI FAYL?
cars.module.ts
«in the ___ context» — qayerni ochish kerak
✅ Tuzatish: cars.module.ts’ni oching → providers: [CarsService]
Saytdagi mentor darsining asosiy usuli. NestJS xatolarining ko‘pi uzun va qo‘rqinchli ko‘rinadi, lekin “can’t resolve dependencies” xabarida doim uchta javob bor. 1) of the ___ — qaysi klass yordam so‘rayapti; 2) argument ___ at index [0] — qaysi dependency topilmadi; 3) in the ___ context — qaysi modul faylini ochish kerak. Animatsiya uchala joyni rangli qilib ko‘rsatadi. Sinfda o‘quvchiga javobni aytmang: xabarni birga o‘qing va “qaysi fayl?” deb so‘rang. Agar dependency nomida Repository so‘zi bo‘lsa (masalan, CarEntityRepository) — modul faylidagi imports’da TypeOrmModule.forFeature([...]) borligini tekshiring.
